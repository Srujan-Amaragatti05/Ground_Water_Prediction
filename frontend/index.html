1. Rigorous Metric Definitions

  • Tolerance-Based Prediction Accuracy: Percentage of predictions within tolerance margin $\delta$: Accuracy(±δ) = (1/N) ∑ I(|y - ŷ| ≤ δ) × 100%. In groundwater regression, conventional classification accuracy is mathematically inapplicable.
  • Held-Out Temporal Split: Training (2016–2021), Validation (2021–2022), Held-Out Testing (2022–2023) ensuring strict temporal separation without look-ahead bias.

2. Architectural Insights

  • GRU Superiority: GRU achieves the lowest RMSE (2.1616 mbgl) with 15,937 parameters due to its streamlined reset and update gating, avoiding gradient saturation on 5-step annual sequences.
  • Hybrid Dual-Branch: Incorporates spatial coordinates via a parallel Dense embedding layer concatenated with temporal GRU representations to condition predictions on geographic location.
